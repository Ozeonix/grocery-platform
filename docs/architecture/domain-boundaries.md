# Domain Boundaries

## Auth
Authentication, sessions, tokens, OTP and credential lifecycle.

## Users
Profiles, addresses, roles and user preferences.

## Stores
Store onboarding, store status, business profile and operating hours.

## Products
Catalog, categories, product metadata and pricing.

## Inventory
Stock levels, reservations, adjustments and inventory history.

## Cart
Customer cart and checkout preparation.

## Orders
Order lifecycle, line items, pricing snapshots and order state.

## Delivery
Delivery assignment, partner state, pickup, tracking and proof of delivery.

## Payments
Payment lifecycle, gateway references, refunds and reconciliation.

## Notifications
Email/SMS/push/in-app notification delivery.

## Support
Tickets, customer issues and operational notes.
